## 7. Method

Every plan number comes from row/column luma scans through the printed chains (frames in §3), thin tread lines from a local-contrast test (pixel < 215 with both neighbours ±2 rows lighter by ≥ 18), render numbers from class scans on the 1280 px renders registered on two anchors per axis, and the section from its five datums. Scripts: `tools/measure.py` (the consolidated scans), `tools/atlas.py`, `tools/build-truth.py`, `tools/diff-v1.py`. They are evaluation tooling under `research/` and are never imported by production code (architecture tests enforce this).
