## GEOMETRY CLOSURE

`packages/geometry/src/closure.ts` — `geometryClosureAudit(model, scene)`.
For every pair of structural solids: shared volume (ray-parity sampling),
same-facing coplanar area (polygon clipping; area under a third solid is
enclosed and not counted), and, for pairs the model says meet within 10 cm,
the gap (vertex-to-triangle distance). The intended relation comes from the
model — CONTACT, BEARING (a plate may share at most wall T × plate thickness ×
run), FLUSH, PENETRATION (a chimney through its roof), GUARDS, SEPARATION —
over the §4 labels: WALL↔SLAB, WALL↔ROOF, WALL↔RETURN_WALL,
MAIN_MASS↔ATTACHED_MASS, BALCONY_SLAB↔WALL, BALCONY_SLAB↔FASCIA,
RAILING↔BALCONY, PARAPET↔FLAT_ROOF, FACADE_FRAME↔HOST_FACADE,
TERRACE↔EXTERIOR_FLOOR_DATUM, TRIM↔ROOF. Railing ends are measured to walls
on their real inward side or to another railing; terraces against their
floor datum; slivers, non-manifold edges and disconnected members per solid.
Findings carry scope EXTERIOR or INTERIOR. Nothing is repaired.

Tests: `packages/geometry/test/closure.test.ts` (15).

## SEMANTIC MATERIAL SYSTEM

`packages/mobile-scene/src/semantics.ts`: 21 semantic groups — WALL_MAIN,
WALL_SECONDARY, WALL_INTERIOR, WALL_CLADDING, ROOF_MAIN, FLAT_ROOF,

compares web and phone mesh for mesh on every sealed candidate).

## SOURCE-DERIVED COLOR HINTS

The chain of §13: registered facade → region mask (the body's own open face,
clear of openings, recesses and nearer bodies; each return's outer face; each
recessed wall column by column) → exclusions (vegetation, sky and glazing) →
brightness relative to the render's own white point → robust median → tone
family (LIGHT ≥ 0.8, DARK < 0.45) → model material → palette rung. No colour
from a render reaches the screen. Auto v3: main body LIGHT (94 % of white),
garage DARK (34 %), the garage's return DARK, every gable-frame return LIGHT;
front recess timber 0.65..3.19 then anthracite 3.19..7.90 (truth 3.25), rear
recess timber. A hint moves a group only to another rung of the palette and
never into a neighbour's luminance (a dark main body would merge with its
roof and is refused). Stress: a hundred board stripes read as at most two
finish runs; a half-timber, half-dark wall as two, split within 0.1 m.
