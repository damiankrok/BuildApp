# 03 — Assumption register (curated across the Council)

The full per-seat registers are in `reviewers/` (A 21, B 23, C 28, D 52, E 16, F 27, G entries). This
is the curated register the implementation decision works from: one row per assumption that decides
an outcome, with a single ID, the seats that found it, and the Council's decision.
Decision vocabulary: **keep** / **soften** (becomes a score or a named partial) / **branch** (becomes
one candidate family among several) / **remove** / **defer** (named debt, next stage).
