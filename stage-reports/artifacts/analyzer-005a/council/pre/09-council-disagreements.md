# 09 — Council disagreements and how each was decided

Seats: A source/evidence, B plan/CV/OCR, C geometry/constraints, D red team, E runtime/progress,
F canonical invariants, G verification/holdouts. Each seat wrote its report without reading the
others'; the orchestrator's own replays were shared as raw evidence (the brief's EVIDENCE UPDATE),
never as conclusions.

| # | Question | Positions | Decision and reason |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | What to do when the dimensioned plan copy was not fetched | **A**: stop before decomposition with a `SOURCE_LIMITED` reason naming the lost copy — "honest partial beats invalid geometry". **C/D + replays**: the surviving area-table copy is the same drawing with correct outer chains; on Kosaćce it resolves (extent and scale exact) | **Try the surviving copy first; name the loss when it does not resolve.** A's diagnostic requirement is adopted in full (lost copies by claimed role in the trace and in `missingEvidence`), A's early stop is not: stopping would refuse a house the phone can build. |
| 2 | Fix the reading layer now (OCR orientation vote, additive scale vote, READ-only anchors) or tolerate it with hypotheses | **B**: these are the root causes of e-OZE; prototype V1 fixes e-OZE 7/7 and leaves Kosaćce/Marcówki ground plans unchanged, but moves the Marcówki attic-853 scale 3.698 → 3.060 (unverifiable). **C/F**: accepted houses must stay byte-identical; the resolver fixes e-OZE from sealed evidence. **D**: "if e-OZE completes but copy/resize invariance still fail, that is a fourth per-project fix" | **Defer the reading-layer change to the next stage, decided on holdout evidence**, and make it the one next step if the holdouts confirm it. | 3 | Scoring: weighted sum or lexicographic buckets | Orchestrator's first draft: weighted sum of footprint, wall continuity, dimension agreement, usable ratio, parsimony. **C**: lexicographic on buckets that are existing constants; within a bucket the footprint residual decides nothing (stops decimal-chasing of the published figure); acceptance needs corroboration | **C adopted.** A weighted sum invents weights (new tuned constants), which D forbids. |
