# 005M licensing matrix — code, weights, training data, derivatives, runtime, redistribution

Read live on 2026-10-07 from the model repositories (Hugging Face API snapshots kept outside the repository), their model
cards and technical reports, the runtimes' LICENSE files and the dataset cards. A research sub-agent gathered the
dataset-card and terms facts and gave a source URL for each. The rows marked † were checked again here against the API
snapshots. The post-review round-1 licensing corrections (`post-review/round1-reviewer-F-licensing-isolation.md`, F-1 to
F-7 and F-12) were checked again on 2026-10-07 against the primary source named at each point: model and dataset cards
at their pinned revisions, the Hugging Face API, the live Gemma Terms and Google's own archive of them, the upstream
licence files and the llama.cpp tree. **This is engineering due diligence, not legal advice. Every commercial route
stays CONDITIONAL on counsel.**

**Apache-2.0 metadata on a model does not settle the training data** (brief §19).
- Every candidate except Gemma carries an Apache-2.0 *weights* tag.
- None of them publishes an itemised, licence-checked list of its training data.
- SmolVLM2 publishes enough to measure its mixture. By its own card, **≈ 77.5 % of the declared mix comes from
  academic-only or non-commercial sets**, and 19.4 % is text generated by Llama-3-70B and Qwen2-72B (§1a).
- A fine-tune keeps its base's provenance. BuildPlan's synthetic data is clean *fine-tuning data*, but it does not
  clean the base it is applied to (§3.5).

counsel question (§4). They are recorded because they are disclosed.

### 1b. Outputs and distillation: may a model's outputs train another model?

None of the candidates' or teachers' own terms forbids it. What differs is what the student becomes and what follows it.

| model (role in 005M) | terms on the weights | may its outputs train another model? | what a student trained on them owes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Qwen3-VL-2B / 4B (candidates) | Apache-2.0† | yes: Apache-2.0 does not govern outputs | nothing under the licence |
| Qwen3-VL-8B (primary teacher, ran, `0c351dd`) / 32B (upper oracle, not run, `0cfaf48`) | Apache-2.0† | yes, as above | nothing under the licence. This choice keeps 005M's soft targets free of output-attribution duties |
| SmolVLM2-2.2B / 500M (candidate; route-A base) | Apache-2.0† | yes | nothing under the licence; its *training-data* provenance (§1a) is a separate question |
| InternVL3.5-2B-Instruct (candidate) | Apache-2.0 weights, MIT code | yes | nothing under its own licence; its own SFT data includes InternVL3-78B outputs (next rows, and §3.3) |
| Gemma 3n E2B / E4B (candidate, not run) | Gemma Terms | yes, but the student is then a **Model Derivative** | the whole Gemma Terms: §3.1 pass-through, the incorporated Prohibited Use Policy, §3.2 remote restriction, §4.5 deletion on termination |
| *contrast:* Qwen-License models (Qwen2.5-72B-Instruct, Qwen2.5-VL-72B-Instruct, InternVL3-78B) | Qwen License (`license: other` / `qwen`) | yes, with a duty | §5(b): "If you use the Materials or any outputs or results therefrom to create, train, fine-tune, or improve an AI model that is distributed or made available, you shall prominently display 'Built with Qwen' or 'Improved using Qwen' in the related product documentation." |
| *contrast:* Llama 3 (Community License); Qwen2 (Tongyi Qianwen licence) | as named | **no**, for "any other large language model" (Llama 3 §1.b.v; Tongyi Qianwen §5(b); quoted in §1a) | not applicable; both sit upstream of SmolVLM2's Magpie text only |
| *contrast:* DeepSeek-R1 (in InternVL3.5's SFT pipeline) | MIT | yes, expressly: "allow for any modifications and derivative works, including, but not limited to, distillation for training other LLMs" | the MIT notice |

The Qwen License §5(b) text was read in `Qwen/Qwen2.5-72B-Instruct/LICENSE@495f393` (line 32) and is word-identical in
`Qwen/Qwen2.5-VL-72B-Instruct/LICENSE@89c8620`. InternVL3-78B links to the former.

Gemma's definition (Terms §1.1(e), live text): "'Model Derivatives' means all (i) modifications to Gemma, (ii) works
based on Gemma, or (iii) any other machine learning model which is created by transfer of patterns of the weights,
parameters, operations, or Output of Gemma, to that model in order to cause that model to perform similarly to Gemma,
including distillation methods that use intermediate data representations or methods based on the generation of
synthetic data Outputs by Gemma for training that model. For clarity, Outputs are not deemed Model Derivatives." §3.3
adds: "Google claims no rights in Outputs you generate using Gemma." So Gemma's outputs are free to keep, but a student
trained on them carries the Gemma Terms. Gemma as a teacher would make a BuildPlan student a Gemma Model Derivative even
on an Apache-2.0 base. The Prohibited Use Policy says nothing about training on outputs.

## 3. What this means for a recommendation

1. **Qwen3-VL (any size).** The cleanest weight licence among the candidates: Apache-2.0, ungated, quantised by the
   publisher. Its data provenance is undisclosed, not known-dirty. Its one named data generator, the re-captioner
   Qwen2.5-VL-32B, is itself Apache-2.0 (tag at `7cfb30d`). A pack needs the Apache-2.0 text and attribution; no NOTICE
   file exists.
2. **SmolVLM2.** The weight licence is clean, but by the card's own table ≈ 77.5 % of the declared mix is NC /
   academic-only, and 19.4 % is Llama-3 / Qwen2-generated text (§1a). That is a counsel item before any shipping use.
   Research use raises no issue. Teacher use that feeds a *shipped* student is the same counsel item, in weaker form.
3. **InternVL3.5.** MIT + Apache-2.0, and its training data was withdrawn from public access. Unlike Qwen3-VL, its SFT
   pipeline is disclosed, and it runs through a Qwen-License model: the model card says the "Thinking" data was built by
   having InternVL3-78B describe each image for DeepSeek-R1. The Qwen License §5(b) asks for "Built with Qwen" or
   "Improved using Qwen" on a model trained on Qwen outputs (§1b). Whether that reaches InternVL3.5's downstream users
   is a counsel question. The `-Instruct` checkpoint that ran is CPT + SFT; MMPR and RL apply only to `-MPO` and the
   plain checkpoint. **If InternVL3.5 is chosen as a pilot, this is a P1 item and goes into the recommendation.**
4. **Gemma 3n.** Shippable only under the Gemma Terms' pass-through obligations, the incorporated Prohibited Use Policy
   (which Google may update), and Google's right to restrict usage that it "reasonably believes" violates the Agreement
   (§1, Gemma row). A fine-tune, a MatFormer slice, or a student trained on Gemma's outputs is a Model Derivative under
   the same terms (§1b). Access is gated and was not available here.
5. **No route needs a real publisher image for training, and no student route is commercial-clean by that fact
   alone.** BuildPlan's synthetic data stays the commercial-clean *fine-tuning data* (005J's conclusion), now with
   generator-exact abstention targets (`student-training.md`). A fine-tune keeps its base's provenance, so a shipped
   student is only as clean as its base.
   - **Route A**: `HuggingFaceTB/SmolVLM2-500M-Video-Instruct@7b375e1b73b11138ff12fe22c8f2822d8fe03467`, the smallest
     pack. It **inherits SmolVLM2's mixture**. Its card at that revision lists the same 12 datasets and the same
     per-source table as the 2.2B card, so ≈ 77.5 % of the declared mix is academic-only / NC (§1a). Route A is **not
     commercial-clean**. It is CONDITIONAL on counsel, and this caveat goes wherever route A is recommended.
   - **Cleaner route-A base (A′)**: `HuggingFaceTB/SmolVLM-500M-Instruct@a7da5b986cb59b408707209984f360a5f4ad7e47`.
     005J called it "the cleaner fine-tuning base of the family" (`analyzer-005j/licensing-matrix.md` §2,
     `training-plan.md`); the full revision is in `analyzer-005j/vlm/vlm-audit.md`.
     - Its card at that revision is Apache-2.0, lists only `HuggingFaceM4/the_cauldron` and `HuggingFaceM4/Docmatix`,
       and says "The training data comes from The Cauldron and Docmatix datasets".
     - It is image-only, and so is this task.
     - It is SmolVLM2-500M's own `base_model`, so A′ is route A's lineage without the SmolVLM2 video and instruction
       layer. The architecture and size are the same (005J found identical ONNX load-set sizes).
     - It is cleaner, not clean. The Cauldron's sub-dataset licences are unaudited (ChartQA GPL-3.0 and Hateful Memes
       behind an agreement are known), and the data of its SmolLM2-360M-Instruct language backbone was not audited.
     - 005M neither ran nor trained it.
   - **Route B** (Qwen3-VL-2B) inherits only "undisclosed" (item 1).
   - **Route C** (Gemma 3n) is a Gemma Model Derivative (item 4).

## 4. Unverified (stated, not assumed)

- Whether a quantised checkpoint is an Apache-2.0 "Derivative Work" or only "Object form". No publisher addresses it;
  the obligations are the same either way for attribution.
- The licence of every Cauldron sub-dataset (only ChartQA and Hateful Memes were checked), and MovieChat-1K's data licence.
  The Cauldron gap also bounds how clean route A′ is (§3.5).
- Whether any Qwen3-VL or InternVL3.5 SFT/RL data is non-commercial. Neither publishes an itemised list.
- Whether the disclosed upstream output clauses bind anyone downstream of a model trained on those outputs. These are
  the Qwen License §5(b) (through InternVL3-78B into InternVL3.5's SFT data), and the Llama 3 Community License §1.b.v
  and Tongyi Qianwen §5(b) (through the Magpie text in SmolVLM2's mix). They are recorded as facts, not conclusions.
- What `other-video/combined` (5.7 % of SmolVLM2's mix) contains; the card does not say. The mapping of the card's
  `magpie_pro_*` names to LLaVA-OneVision-Data configs is inferred.
- The training data of SmolLM2-360M-Instruct, the language backbone of both route A and route A′.
- The licence of KleidiAI, which llama.cpp downloads only when `GGML_CPU_KLEIDIAI` is enabled (§1c).
- The meaning of `gated: "manual"` for Gemma 3n against the page's "requests are processed immediately". Here, the
  request was simply refused without a credential.
