## Full findings

### R2A-1 (P2) — F4 probes the platform across the whole zone, not across the bodies standing on its back (the frozen span), so a partial-width platform is still dropped silently

Scenario: Frozen G2 called readTerraceExtension with [recess.spanFrom, recess.spanTo] = from..to of the bodies whose face reaches the zone's back (recesses.ts:166). F4 uses the zone's full along-extent instead (= the envelope side). readTerraceExtension needs a line row covering 85% of [span0+0.1, span1-0.1]. Take the 'ell' fixture (main body 0..17 m on the back of the front strip, wing 17..30 m set back 7.65 m) with a hatched platform outlined in front of the main body only. The frozen base builds the extension: the outline covers 100% of 0.1..16.9. Under F4 the outline covers about 57% of 0.1..29.9, so ext is undefined: no terrace and no gap. That is the TOPOLOGY-1 silent drop that F4 was supposed to close. The report's 'A platform the plan outlines beyond such a strip is named, never built (F4)' (STAGE report §C) is therefore true only for platforms spanning about 85% of the whole side. Family 15(b) has a single full-width body and cannot tell the two spans apart. A related point: F4 also probes strips that no open body stands on (the PERFORMANCE-3 case), and the reason then says 'beyond a wall the body was cut short of' with no such body.

Suggested fix: Probe over the span the frozen code used: the along-extent of the bodies standing on the zone's back at storey 0, or of the open bodies (openFacesOf), or the covered stretches.
