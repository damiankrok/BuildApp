### P1

**H-01 — The inspector is not solid to the finger: taps on it pick the model and press the rail.** Inspector.kt:79–87 — the root `Column` has `.background(Palette.Sheet)` and no pointer input; unlike `GlassSurface` (Glass.kt:45–52) and M3 `Surface`, it does not take part in hit testing. Compose stops at the front-most sibling only where that sibling has a pointer-input node, so the sheet's title/storey text (PanelHeader, :93–98), the 1 dp hairline (:165) and the empty right part of the footer row (:166–177, the Row wraps its two buttons and is not `fillMaxWidth`) let the touch through to what is behind: the Viewport's `pointerInput` → a tap becomes a pick → `onPicked(null)` clears the selection and `LaunchedEffect(selected)` closes the inspector, or `Hit` swaps it to whatever geometry lies under the sheet; and, in portrait, the rail sits at y ≈ 604–858 under the sheet (see H-08), so the footer's blank right end (x > ≈ 265, y 810–866) lands on "Dopasuj" (y 794–854) — the camera refits with the sheet still open. Same at the side panel's footer in landscape. Fix: lift GlassSurface's swallow into `fun Modifier.solidToFinger() = pointerInput(Unit) { awaitEachGesture { do { val e = awaitPointerEvent() } while (e.changes.any { it.pressed }) } }` in Glass.kt and apply it to the Inspector's root modifier (first in the chain). **H-02 — Landscape: the tool pane lies under the timeline; the cycle-1 P1-3 fix is wrong on its side.** HouseWorkspace.kt:273: `paneMax = if (railAtFoot) maxHeight − topDp − bottomDp − 16 else maxHeight − topDp − 16`.
