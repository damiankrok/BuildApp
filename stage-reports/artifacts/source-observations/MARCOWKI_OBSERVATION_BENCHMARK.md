### FACADE_FRAME_SOLIDS — SOURCE_SUPPORTS_MORE

**Owner's finding.** the characteristic front/rear facade carries thick real 3D frame/beam-like solids that the model under-models

**What the sources carry.** 138 linear members carry a depth cue (front 4, rear 4, side 72, renders 58); 67 further bands carry none and are recorded as surface regions

**What the model asserts.** the reference model carries the same facade zones as SURFACE_REGIONs — finish assignments on a flat wall — and no solid linear member anywhere on the front or rear

**Note.** this is the owner’s finding, recovered from the images rather than asserted: each member names the cue that promoted it, and the bands that carry only a change of tone are kept apart from those that do not

### FRAME_CONTINUITY — SOURCE_SUPPORTS_MORE

**Owner's finding.** the major facade frame appears to continue from the garage zone toward/under the balcony and along facade edges

**What the sources carry.** the front elevation’s longest member spans most of the facade width and CONTINUES_ACROSS relations link horizontal members to the vertical ones they meet

**What the model asserts.** the reference model has no element that runs across the garage/house junction: the zones are separate finish regions on separate walls

**Note.** a CONTINUES_ACROSS relation is what lets a later solver recover ONE member turning a corner instead of two unrelated lumps

### BALCONY_SIDE_RETURNS — SOURCE_SILENT

**Owner's finding.** balcony/loggia side walls/returns are incomplete

**What the sources carry.** 18 recess mouths found; 0 side returns found closing them; every side with no return found is recorded as a named gap

**What the model asserts.** the reference model’s loggia is a recess in the wall with no modelled return walls

**Note.** a recess with no side returns is a hole in a wall rather than a room, which is exactly what the preview showed

### STAIR_TOPOLOGY — SOURCE_SILENT

**Owner's finding.** the staircase topology/orientation does not match the published plan closely enough

**What the sources carry.** NO tread run on any published plan could be held to be a staircase: 5 regular run(s) of strokes were considered and each was rejected with its reason. The section yields 1 stepped chain(s)

**What the model asserts.** the reference model asserts a 4-riser flight, 4 winders and a 9-riser flight (17 risers at 0.18 m)

**Note.** the published plans are 853 px for a 12 m house, so a 0.27 m going is about 9 px and a fill pattern is 4 px. At that separation a hatch and a flight are not distinguishable, and a stair invented from a planting symbol cannot be undone downstream. The model’s stair is therefore an assertion the current sources do not corroborate — which is the finding, not a defect in it

### ROOF_PITCH — AGREED

**Owner's finding.** not raised; checked because it is the one angle the model claims to have measured

**What the sources carry.** 36 roof edges measured in image space, closest to the model within 0.04 degrees

**What the model asserts.** the reference model uses a 40 degree pitch from the section’s printed annotation

**Note.** an image-space angle is a true pitch only on a drawing with the same scale in both axes; this corroborates rather than measures
